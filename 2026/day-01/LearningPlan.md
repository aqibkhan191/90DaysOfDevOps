## 🧠 My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud Engineering

DevOps is a cultural and technical movement that bridges the gap between software development and IT operations. It emphasizes automation, collaboration, continuous delivery, and fast feedback loops. Cloud Engineering is the practice of designing, building, and managing infrastructure and applications on cloud platforms (like AWS, Azure, or GCP) using modern tools and DevOps principles.

Together, DevOps and Cloud enable teams to ship reliable software faster, at scale.
